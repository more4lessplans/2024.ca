Fix Your 5G/LTE Internet Connection

Quick Settings Checklist

  • Is your APN set correctly? An incorrect or missing APN is the most common cause of a connection that won't work at all.
  • Is Band Lock set to "All" or "Auto"? If it's locked to a specific band that's no longer available in your area, your connection can disconnect entirely.
  • Is Network mode set to Auto or Manual? On Manual, if your selected carrier becomes unavailable, the connection can drop. On Auto, the device picks a network for you — but it doesn't always choose the strongest one. If you already know which carrier gives you the best signal at your address, Manual is usually the better choice.
Not sure where to find these settings? Follow the guide for your device — Nighthawk APN, Nighthawk Band Locking, Nighthawk Manual Network, or Orbi APN and Orbi Manual Network.

Check Your Cell Signal First

Our service runs on the same cellular network as your phone. Check whether your phone also has weak or no signal in the same spot — if so, the issue is likely the local network rather than your device.

  1. Restart Your Device
    • Unplug the device or turn it off for 10 seconds.
    • Plug it back in or turn it on, wait for a full restart, then try connecting again.
  2. Improve Signal Strength & Placement
    • Place the hub near a window, ideally on a higher floor and as close as possible to the nearest known cell tower.
    • Avoid basements, cabinets, or metal objects that can block signal.
    • Try a few different spots — small changes in placement can meaningfully change signal strength, so trial and error is worth it.
    • Check the hub's LEDs or app for signal strength. Aim for at least 3/5 bars.
  3. Know About Network Issues
    • Cell tower maintenance may cause temporary outages or slowdowns.
    • Peak hours (evenings/weekends) can slow speeds due to high network use.
  4. Recheck the Settings Checklist Above
    • Confirm the APN, Band Lock, and Network mode settings against the checklist at the top of this guide.
    • Try switching to another major Canadian network provider for a stronger signal.
  5. Inspect the SIM Card
    • Turn off and unplug the device.
    • Remove the SIM card, clean it gently with a soft cloth, and reinsert it securely.
    • Turn the device back on and check the connection.
  6. Test Your Speed
    • Measure your internet speed using a speed test tool.
    • Test at different times (morning, afternoon, evening).
    • Try moving the hub to different spots in your home.

Internet Too Slow?

  1. Choose a Stronger Network
    • Log into your router's settings via its app or web interface.
    • Find "Network Selection" or "Band Selection."
    • Select the strongest band or set to "Auto" for best performance.
    Nighthawk: Band Locking Guide Orbi: Manual Network Guide
  2. Optimize Hub Placement
    • Place the hub near a window or outside wall, toward the nearest cell tower.
    • Avoid thick walls, metal objects, or appliances.
    • Elevate the hub (e.g., on a shelf) away from other electronics.
    • Test different locations with a speed test tool.
    Run Speed Test
  3. Use a MIMO Antenna
    • Consider a 2x2 MIMO antenna from retailers like Amazon.
    • Aim it toward the nearest cell tower or strongest signal direction.
    • This can improve signal strength and speed.
  4. Avoid Peak Usage Times
    • Switch to another network (see step 1) if speeds are slow.
    • Use the internet during off-peak hours for better performance.

Troubleshoot a Router That Won't Power On

  1. For Mobile Devices with a Battery
    • Remove the battery from the device.
    • Connect it to a power source using a USB-C cable and 3.0A power adapter (without the battery).
    • Wait 1–2 minutes and check for indicator lights or error codes on the screen.
    Nighthawk Battery-Free Guide
  2. For Home Devices with a Power Adapter
    • Ensure the power adapter is securely connected to the router and a working outlet.
    • Try a different outlet to rule out power issues.
    • Use a compatible adapter with the same voltage if available.
    • Check for any indicator lights on the device.
  3. Reset the Device (Caution)
    • Do not reset mobile devices unless instructed, as it may disconnect service.
    • For home devices, find the reset pinhole and press it with a paperclip while powered on.
    Orbi Factory Reset Guide
  4. Check for Other Issues
    • Inspect cables and replace if possible.
    • Confirm other devices work on the same outlet.
    • Look for damage, burn marks, or unusual smells on the device.

Change Your Wi-Fi Name or Password

Option 1: Use the NETGEAR App (Recommended)

  • For Nighthawk M1/M6, use the NETGEAR Mobile app.
  • For Orbi, use the NETGEAR Orbi app.
  • Download the app from the App Store or Google Play.
  • Open the app, connect to your device, and go to Wi-Fi Settings to update the name (SSID) or password.
  • Save changes, which apply immediately.
Download App

Option 2: Use a Web Browser

  1. Connect to Your Device
    • Connect to your device's Wi-Fi network.
  2. Access Settings Page
    • Open a browser and type 192.168.1.1 in the address bar.
  3. Log In
    • Nighthawk: Username admin, Password attadmin
    • Orbi: Username admin, Password more4less
  4. Update Settings
    • Go to Wireless or Wi-Fi Settings.
    • Update the Network Name (SSID) or Password, then save.
    • Devices may need to reconnect.
Prefer a full walkthrough with screenshots? See the Orbi Wi-Fi Name & Password guide.

Troubleshoot Website Access Issues

Why This Happens

  • Canadian Content Limits: Some Canadian apps or websites may not load or show restricted content.
  • U.S. Content Access: You may access U.S.-exclusive platforms (e.g., Hulu, Peacock shows) as a benefit.

Solution: Use a VPN

  • Set your IP address to appear in Canada.
  • Improve online privacy and security.
  • Access both Canadian and U.S. content easily.

Device Won't Stay Connected (Hardware)

Hardware troubleshooting looks a little different between our two device types. Select yours below for targeted steps.

Nighthawk Battery & Power Issues

  1. Bypass the Battery
    • Try running the device plugged in directly, with the battery removed.
    • Test with a high-powered USB-C adapter (3.0A or higher), both with and without the battery installed, to isolate whether the battery is the problem.
  2. Charge While Powered Off
    • Plug the device in while it's turned on, then switch it off while it stays connected to the charger.
    • Leave it plugged in for a couple of hours before powering it back on.
  3. Reinsert the Battery While Plugged In
    • With the device already connected to power, try inserting the battery rather than powering on with it already seated.
View Battery-Free Setup Guide

Orbi Connectivity Issues

Most Orbi connection drops come down to network selection rather than a hardware fault. If you're losing connection or seeing a weak signal, switching from Automatic to Manual network selection lets you lock onto the strongest carrier in your area instead of waiting for the device to reselect one.
