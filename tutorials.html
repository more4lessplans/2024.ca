Please do not factory reset your Nighthawk. Doing so will erase the custom network settings that make your unlimited plan work, and will require manual reprogramming to restore your service.

Quick Tips
Best Placement
Keep your Nighthawk near a window or on an upper floor for the strongest signal. Avoid metal cabinets, thick concrete walls, and large appliances.
Battery & Power
The battery ships pre-installed and charged. Running the device plugged in 24/7? Remove the battery for long-term reliability — see the Battery-Free tab below.
Powering On
Hold the power button for about 3 seconds until the display lights up, then allow up to 60 seconds for the modem to connect to the network.
Signal Fluctuating?
If speeds seem inconsistent, try the Manual Network or Band Locking tutorials below to lock onto the strongest local carrier and band.
Setup Tutorials

Follow these steps to update the APN on your Nighthawk for reliable connectivity.

  1. Download the NETGEAR Mobile App: If you haven't already, download and install the NETGEAR Mobile app.
  2. Connect to Your Router: Ensure your phone is connected to the Nighthawk's Wi-Fi network.
  3. Router Admin Login: Log in with the credentials below.
  4. Access Mobile Settings: Navigate to Settings > Advanced > Mobile > APN.
  5. Add a New APN: Tap Add to create a new profile.
  6. Enter APN Details: Type the required APN provided in your activation email and leave other fields blank.
  7. Save & Restart: Tap Save and reboot your Nighthawk router.

Manually select a network if you are experiencing signal fluctuations.

  1. Connect to Wi-Fi: Ensure your device is connected to the Nighthawk.
  2. Router Admin Login: Use the credentials below to sign in.
  3. Access Mobile Settings: Go to Settings > Advanced > Mobile.
  4. Network Selection: Switch from Auto to Manual.
  5. Choose Carrier: Select your preferred local carrier from the list.
  6. Apply Settings: Save changes and restart the device.

Lock specific LTE bands to prevent switching and improve stability.

  1. Connect to Router: Connect to the router's Wi-Fi network.
  2. Router Admin Login: Use the credentials below.
  3. Access Mobile Settings: Navigate to Settings > Advanced > Mobile.
  4. Select LTE Bands: Tap LTE Bands.
  5. Choose Preferred Band: Select the band that provides the best speeds in your area.
  6. Apply: Save the settings and reboot.

Optimize device longevity for home use by removing the battery.

  1. Power Off: Completely shut down the Nighthawk and unplug the USB cable.
  2. Remove Battery: Remove the back cover and carefully take out the battery.
  3. High-Power AC: Plug the USB cable back in using a high-quality wall adapter (2A minimum).
  4. Power On: Press and hold the power button. The unit will boot without a battery, staying cool and protecting the internal cells from swelling.

Adjust Wi-Fi transmit power based on your current environment.

  1. Router Admin Login: Access the dashboard with the credentials below.
  2. Navigate to Wi-Fi Options: Go to Settings > Wi-Fi > Options.
  3. Wi-Fi Range: Choose Short for maximum battery savings (best for travel) or Long for maximum coverage (best for hotel rooms or small cabins).
  4. Save: Apply settings to update your transmit power.

Quick Tips
Best Placement
Place the Orbi near a window or on an upper floor. Avoid basements, metal enclosures, and locations near large appliances that can weaken the cellular signal.
Connecting Power
Connect the included adapter to the power port on the rear of the Orbi and plug it into a wall outlet. Make sure the rear power switch is on — the LED ring lights up as it starts.
Finding Your Wi-Fi Password
Your network name and password are printed on the label on the bottom of the router. Use these credentials to connect your phones, laptops, and smart devices.
Device Capacity
The Orbi supports up to 64 connected devices at once, so the whole household can stay online together.
Setup Tutorials

Update the APN on your Orbi router for optimal connectivity.

  1. Open the Orbi App: Launch the NETGEAR Orbi app on your smartphone.
  2. Connect to Wi-Fi: Ensure your device is connected to your Orbi's network.
  3. Router Admin Login: When prompted, use the credentials below.
  4. Go to Router Settings: Tap the menu icon and navigate to Settings > Advanced > Mobile > APN.
  5. Add a New APN: Tap Add and enter the APN provided in your activation email.
  6. Save & Restart: Tap Save or Apply and reboot your router.

Follow these steps to manually select a carrier if your signal is unstable.

  1. Open the Orbi App: Ensure you are logged in and connected to the router.
  2. Router Admin Login: Use the credentials below.
  3. Access Mobile Settings: Navigate to Settings > Advanced > Mobile.
  4. Network Selection: Tap Network Selection and switch from Auto to Manual.
  5. Choose Carrier: Select your preferred carrier (e.g., Rogers, Bell, or Telus) from the list.
  6. Apply & Restart: Save your settings and reboot the Orbi.
